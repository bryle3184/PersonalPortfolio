02About & principles

I care about the objects people live with — the screens, systems, and printed things that shape the texture of a working day.

I'm Mira — a product designer and art director in New York. For the last decade I've helped startups, studios, and cultural institutions turn complex problems into interfaces that feel warm, exact, and quietly human. The overlap of editorial craft and product thinking is where I do my best work: interfaces with a point of view, identities with a spine, systems that hold a thousand screens together without crushing the first one.
